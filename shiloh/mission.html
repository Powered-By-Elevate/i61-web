Where gathering builds people.

Book the room and the room does not pocket the money. It spends it on the week that follows.

The problem we work on

Young adults are leaving small towns unanchored — or staying in them adrift.

Not for lack of talent. For lack of people close enough to teach a trade, a habit and a faith at the same table. Shiloh exists to be that table.

What changes with mentorship

Sent, not just launched.

A young adult with no one close by learns by trial, mostly error, and often leaves. One who is known — taught a trade, fed weekly, corrected kindly, prayed for by name — tends to stay, build and bring the next one along. The difference is not talent. It is proximity.

The name

“… that they may be called oaks of righteousness, the planting of the Lord.”

Isaiah 61 — rebuild the ruined places, and do it by raising up the people who live there.
