The problem we work on

Young adults are leaving small towns unanchored — or staying in them adrift.

Not for lack of talent. For lack of people close enough to teach a trade, a habit and a faith at the same table.

What changes with mentorship

Sent, not just launched.

A young adult with no one close by learns by trial, mostly error, and often leaves. One who is known — taught a trade, fed weekly, corrected kindly — tends to stay, build and bring the next one along. The difference is proximity.

“Oaks of righteousness, the planting of the Lord.”

Isaiah 61 — rebuild the ruined places, and do it by raising up the people who live there.
