Our mission

Equip small but strong local initiatives — and keep the relationship at the centre.

Works led by people who know their town, love it, and have already started. Change that lasts comes from people who do life together in a place.

Plainly, then

What i61 is — and is not.

i61 is

✓ An equipping mechanism for local works already under way

✓ Built on relationships that outlast any single project

✓ A supplier of skills, tools, counsel and connections

✓ Small on purpose, and intending to stay that way

i61 is not

✗ A headquarters, a franchise or a network with a logo pack

✗ A grant portal with an application form

✗ The owner or operator of the works it serves

✗ Finished when the money is spent — we stay

Where we are

Early, and honest about it.

Project i61 is organizing as a nonprofit in South Carolina; our federal tax-status determination is in process. Our first connection project is live in Dawsonville, Georgia.

Built to lift

The tagline is the test: if what we do does not lift the people doing the work, we stop doing it.
