Project i61

We help the people who are already building.

Project i61 is an equipping mechanism for small, strong local initiatives — the bridge between people with a work in their hands and the resources, skills and friends that work needs.

Connect With Us Why We Exist
The model

We don't build our own centres. We come alongside.

01

Find the builders

Every town has people already doing the work — a teacher, a tradesman, a pastor with a warehouse and a vision. We look for them, not for markets.

02

Come alongside

Money is the least of it. Skills, tools, counsel, connections and stubborn friendship — whatever the work actually needs, for as long as it needs it.

03

Stay small on purpose

Relationships are the core of the mission. Small equipping stations change a town's culture; institutions mostly change their own org charts.

The name

“… that they may be called oaks of righteousness, the planting of the Lord.”

Isaiah 61 — the chapter this work is named for. Rebuild the ruined places, and do it by raising up the people who live there. The oak on our seal grows above the line and roots below it: what holds is what nobody sees.

First connection project

Shiloh Collective, Dawsonville.

An equipping centre for young adults, built by hand inside a 2,800 square foot warehouse — led by the people of Transforming Truth Ministries. i61 does not run it. We come alongside the people who do.

How we carry ourselves

Hospitality

Gratitude

We build like people who were handed something — because we were.

Generosity

Capacity gets shared. What we help build belongs to its town, not to us.
